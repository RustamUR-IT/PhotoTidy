## System Optimizer 1.3

Sorts your photos, files, apps – and now your home screen. Everything runs on your phone – nothing is uploaded, no costs.
(Formerly "Photo Tidy". Installs as an update and keeps your settings.)

**Install:** tap **PhotoTidy.apk** below on your phone → allow installing from your browser → Install.

### New: smart widgets (your home screen stays as it is)
- **App dashboard** widget: your most-used apps on top, every other app grouped below (Chats, Social, Media, Money, Travel…)
- **Smart folder** widget: pick one folder – it fills itself, most-used apps first; tap its title to switch folder
- Both keep up with new and removed apps and re-sort by how much you use them (with "Usage access")
- Add them from the app (**Home screen → Add dashboard / Add folder**) or long-press your home screen → Widgets → System Optimizer
- Your existing widgets and icons are never moved

### Optional: own home screen
- If you want everything sorted fully automatically: **Home screen → Try it** makes System Optimizer your home app
  (replaces the Nothing home screen, widgets need adding again once; switch back anytime)

### Also new
- Renamed to **System Optimizer**
- **Crash reports**: if the app ever closes unexpectedly, Settings → Problems lets you send the report
- Logic tests run on every build – a version that fails them is never published

### Photos
- Sorts photos into `Pictures/Sorted` (People, Pets, Food, Nature, City & Travel, Vehicles, Documents) and trip/party event folders
- Finds blurry, dark, blank, duplicate photos and old screenshots

### Files
- Sorts loose files into `Documents/Sorted`: Invoices & Receipts, Tickets & Travel, Contracts & Official, Bank & Insurance, Health, Work & School (reads PDFs, German + English)
- Finds old APKs, unfinished/duplicate downloads, empty files; own 30-day trash

### Apps
- What every app does, Important/Essential badges, reminder before removing banking, login-code, password, chat, email or ID apps
- Unused apps, batch uninstall, shortcut to Disable / Force stop
